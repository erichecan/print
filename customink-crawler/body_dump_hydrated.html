It's a midweight, 4.5 oz. ringspun cotton tee, surprisingly soft to be so easy on your group's event budget. And it's available in the Gildan line-up of colors. (The proverbial box of crayons you begged for as a kid, i.e. more than 50!) And speaking of kids, we have custom t-shirts in sizes for them, teens, and adults.

Updated to fit today's fashion, the shoulder seam on these Gildan Softstyle Jersey T-shirts has been moved slightly forward to take advantage of your body's natural position and increase comfort.
