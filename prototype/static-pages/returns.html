Returns & Refunds

Your satisfaction is our priority. Here's how we make returns easy.

100% Satisfaction Guarantee

We stand behind every product. If you're not completely satisfied with your order, we'll make it right.

Return Eligibility

Items That Can Be Returned

  • Standard products with manufacturing defects
  • Incorrect items received
  • Damaged items

Items That Cannot Be Returned

  • Custom-designed items (unless defective or incorrect)
  • Items worn or used
  • Personalized products
  • Items returned after 30 days

Return Process

  1. Contact us: Email returns@suvernireplus.com or call 800-293-4232
  2. Get authorization: We'll provide a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number
  3. Pack items: Use original packaging if possible
  4. Ship back: Send to the address provided within 7 days
  5. Receive refund: Processed within 5-10 business days after we receive your return

Refund Timeline

  • Return received: 3-5 business days processing
  • Refund approved: Credit/debit refunds process in 5-10 business days
  • PayPal refunds: 3-5 business days

Return Conditions

To be eligible for return, items must:

  • Be unworn and unused
  • Include original tags
  • Be in original packaging
  • Include RMA number on package

Shipping Costs

Free Returns

We cover return shipping costs for defective, damaged, or incorrect items.

Customer Responsibility

For size exchanges or change of mind returns, return shipping is the customer's responsibility.

Exchanges

Need a different size? Contact us within 30 days to arrange an exchange.

  • Same item in different size
  • Price difference refunded or charged
  • Shipping costs apply

Damaged or Defective Items

Received a damaged item? We're sorry! Contact us immediately and we'll send a replacement at no cost.

Contact: quality@suvernireplus.com or 800-293-4232

Order Cancellations

You can cancel your order if:

  • Production hasn't started (full refund)
  • Production has begun (50% refund)
  • Order has shipped (standard return policy applies)

Our customer service team is ready to assist with returns and exchanges.
