Size Guide

Find the perfect fit for your custom apparel

How to Measure

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, under your arms

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips

Fit Notes

  • Regular Fit: Standard sizing for comfortable everyday wear
  • Relaxed Fit: More room in chest and waist area
  • Slim Fit: Tapered for a more fitted look
  • Long Length: Extra length available in XL and above

Still Unsure?

If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for comfort. Our customer service team is happy to help you choose the right size.

Pro Tip

Tip: If you have a shirt that fits you well, lay it flat and measure from seam to seam across the chest. Compare this measurement to our size chart to find your perfect fit.
